    ChavesNaMao City Property Listings Extractor: CSV and Excel export

    Search ChavesNaMão for sale or rental properties in 15 major Brazilian cities, with price, condominium fee, IPTU, room counts, area and neighborhood for each listing.

    This tutorial runs ChavesNaMao City Property Listings Extractor through the Apify API and saves what it returns as a CSV file, an Excel workbook or JSON, keeping the 8 columns its publisher puts first: title, price, priceCurrency, propertyType, bedrooms, areaSqM, neighborhood and city.
