    Europages European Manufacturer & Supplier Finder: CSV and Excel export

    Search Europages for European manufacturers, suppliers and service providers by product keyword, category or country, and get company name, location, employee range, product categories, certifications, phone and website per match.

    This tutorial runs Europages European Manufacturer & Supplier Finder through the Apify API and saves what it returns as a CSV file, an Excel workbook or JSON, keeping the 6 columns its publisher puts first: companyName, country, city, employees, products and companyUrl.

    What people export it for

    • Supplier sourcing — shortlist manufacturers or distributors for a product category in a target country
    • Lead list building — collect company name, phone and website for outbound sales in a European market
    • Market mapping — count and compare how many listed suppliers exist per country for a product line
    • Certification screening — filter prospective suppliers down to ones advertising ISO/CE certification
    • Profile refresh — re-fetch a known set of companyUrls to check for updated contact details

    Fix common problems

    Why are employees, phone or certifications missing on some records?

    Europages only shows these fields when the company itself filled them in on its profile; the actor never fills in a guessed or placeholder value.

    HTTP 401 means the token is missing; an empty file usually means the input matched nothing, which the run log in Console names.
