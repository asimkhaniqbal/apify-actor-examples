    What people export it for

    • Content research: see which posts LinkedIn's editors rank first in a topic, with exact dates and full text.
    • Influencer discovery: collect authors, follower counts, and public Influencer badges from a niche.
    • PR and media prospecting: build a list of Top Voices honorees with profile URLs and headlines.
    • Benchmarking: compare reactions, comments, and media formats across topics and time windows.
    • Corpus building: export complete posts with their top comments for analysis or RAG pipelines.

    Fix common problems

    Why did I get fewer posts than maxPosts?

    A topic page holds only 9–10 posts, duplicates across topics are removed, and your filters may remove more. Increase maxDepth, add topics or startUrls, or loosen the filters.

    Why is my date filter empty?

    The curated directory often has no posts inside 24 hours, 7 days, or 30 days. Try 90d or 1y, or leave the date filter empty.

    Why are reposts, reaction breakdowns, or views missing?

    Those values are not published on the public pages. They are omitted instead of being returned as zero.

    HTTP 401 means the token is missing; an empty file usually means the input matched nothing, which the run log in Console names.
