    Fix common problems

    Do I need cookies, an account or a proxy?

    No. Every supported field is public and cookieless. The actor never asks for credentials.

    Why does browseCategory now work outside the US?

    The curated category paths are stable US catalog paths, and Nike's public wall API accepts them with any supported marketplace/language pair. Prices and URLs come back localized.

    Why are only three reviews returned?

    Nike's public review endpoint exposes a rating summary, an AI summary and the three most recent reviews per style. The separate review actor in the Store covers deep review pagination.

    More from the publisher:

    • Why is a field missing from one record?

    HTTP 401 means the token is missing; an empty file usually means the input matched nothing, which the run log in Console names.
