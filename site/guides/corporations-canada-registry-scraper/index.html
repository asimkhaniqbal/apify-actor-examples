    Corporations Canada Registry Scraper: export to CSV, Excel or JSON

    Look up federally incorporated Canadian companies on Corporations Canada (ised-isde.canada.ca). Search by corporate name with province/status/act filters, or fetch exact records by corporation number, business number (BN), or internal corporation ID.

    This tutorial runs Corporations Canada Registry Scraper through the Apify API and saves what it returns as a CSV file, an Excel workbook or JSON, keeping the 8 columns its publisher puts first: corpName, corpNumber, businessNumber, status, statusCategory, isActive, governingLegislation and registeredOfficeProvince.
