    What people export it for

    • Voice of customer: export review text for sentiment, topic, and complaint analysis.
    • Rating monitoring: track average rating, histogram, and the "Customers say" summary across a watch-list.
    • Competitive research: compare products from a keyword or category with one run.
    • Storefront comparison: see which reviews a product carries on other Amazon marketplaces.
    • Quality triage: filter to critical or media reviews to find recurring defects quickly.

    Fix common problems

    Do I need an Amazon account or a proxy?

    No. The actor works with public product data and chooses its own connection automatically. cookieString is optional and only for users who want to paginate their own session.

    Why do some products return fewer rows than others?

    Amazon gates the product-page block probabilistically and the extended feed only covers a subset of ASINs. The actor retries and reports what it actually collected; it never invents rows.

    Why is my star filter returning zero results?

    Filters run over the merged set, so a rating that is rare on the product page and absent from the feed can legitimately return nothing. The dataset then contains an amazon_reviews_no_star_match status record.

    HTTP 401 means the token is missing; an empty file usually means the input matched nothing, which the run log in Console names.
