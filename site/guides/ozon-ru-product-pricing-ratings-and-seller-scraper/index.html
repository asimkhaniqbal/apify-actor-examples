    Fix common problems

    Do I need an Ozon account, cookies, or a proxy?

    No. The actor reads Ozon's public storefront data without credentials, and you do not configure any proxy.

    Why is brand sometimes missing?

    Ozon often shows a trust badge such as "Оригинал" instead of a real brand name. The actor only emits brand when Ozon publishes an explicit brand value and never guesses it from the title.

    Why is deliveryInfo often missing while deliveryText appears?

    Product pages frequently carry an empty delivery widget. deliveryText comes from a visible listing or buybox estimate; deliveryInfo only appears when the product page itself publishes delivery text.

    More from the publisher:

    • Why are pros and cons usually absent?
    • Why do deep results under a non-default sort sometimes look less relevant?

    HTTP 401 means the token is missing; an empty file usually means the input matched nothing, which the run log in Console names.
