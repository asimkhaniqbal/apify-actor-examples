    Global Sources Verified Supplier & Product Finder: CSV and Excel export

    Search Global Sources products or suppliers, or browse a product category, and get price, minimum order quantity, supplier name, country and audited/verified status for each match. Optional filter for verified suppliers only.

    This tutorial runs Global Sources Verified Supplier & Product Finder through the Apify API and saves what it returns as a CSV file, an Excel workbook or JSON, keeping the 7 columns its publisher puts first: title, price, minOrder, supplier, supplierCountry, verifiedStatus and productUrl.

    What people export it for

    • Sourcing shortlists — pull a first-pass list of manufacturers for a product you want to import
    • Verified-supplier vetting — filter straight to audited suppliers before reaching out
    • Category price scanning — browse a whole category to see the spread of prices and MOQs
    • Competitive intelligence — see which suppliers are listing a specific product keyword and at what price
    • Procurement research — compare minimum order quantities across multiple suppliers before negotiating

    Fix common problems

    Why do some products show a price range instead of one number?

    Global Sources often publishes a price range tied to order quantity tiers; the actor returns exactly what's shown on the listing rather than picking one number.

    Why are some supplier records missing products?

    That field only appears when the supplier's listing shows its product categories; not every profile publishes this.

    HTTP 401 means the token is missing; an empty file usually means the input matched nothing, which the run log in Console names.
