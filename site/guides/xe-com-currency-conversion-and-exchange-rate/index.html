    XE.com Currency Conversion & Exchange Rate Scraper: CSV and Excel export

    Convert an amount between currencies, pull a full exchange-rate table for a base currency on any date since the late 1990s, or look up a currency's issuing country, symbol and central bank, for 180+ fiat and crypto currencies.

    This tutorial runs XE.com Currency Conversion & Exchange Rate Scraper through the Apify API and saves what it returns as a CSV file, an Excel workbook or JSON, keeping the 8 columns its publisher puts first: recordType, fromCurrency, toCurrency, amount, convertedAmount, exchangeRate, date and sourceUrl.
