    Yahoo Auctions Japan Keyword & Category Price Scraper: CSV and Excel export

    Search Yahoo Auctions Japan by keyword or browse a category, with current bid price, buy-it-now price, condition, bid count, end time and seller, filtered by price range and sorted by ending soonest, price or bids.

    This tutorial runs Yahoo Auctions Japan Keyword & Category Price Scraper through the Apify API and saves what it returns as a CSV file, an Excel workbook or JSON, keeping the 8 columns its publisher puts first: itemId, title, currentPrice, currency, buyItNowPrice, bids, watchCount and condition.

    Fix common problems

    Why do some rows have no buy-it-now price?

    Only fixed-price listings and auctions that offer instant purchase have one; the field is omitted otherwise.

    Why are some prices 0 or 1 yen?

    Those are live auctions with a low starting bid and no bids yet. Check bids and startPrice to tell them apart.

    HTTP 401 means the token is missing; an empty file usually means the input matched nothing, which the run log in Console names.
