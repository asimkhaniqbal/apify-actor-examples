    What people export it for

    • Job seekers & career sites — aggregate fresh listings by role, category, location, or remote/hybrid arrangement, with the full ad body
    • Recruiters & staffing agencies — monitor competitor job postings, screening questions, and applicant demand
    • Labor market research — track hiring demand and posted salary ranges across categories and regions in HK/TH
    • HR tech / salary benchmarking tools — collect structured salary ranges plus company industry, size and perks
    • Lead generation — find companies actively hiring in a given industry
    • Competitor/company tracking — pull every current listing from a specific employer with mode=browseByCompany
    • Shortlist refresh — re-check saved job URLs for expiry, updated content and current applicant volume

    Fix common problems

    Why is descriptionText missing on some jobs?

    A small number of advertisers publish only an external apply link. When the ad has no body, the field is omitted rather than filled with a placeholder.

    Why is salaryLabel missing on some jobs?

    Not every advertiser discloses a salary. The field is only included when JobsDB displays one.

    Why is salaryMinValue/salaryMaxValue missing even though salaryLabel is present?

    A handful of advertisers enter free text instead of a number (e.g. Negotiable, Competitive, Attractive Salary Package). The structured fields are only added when salaryLabel is confidently a numeric amount or range — never a guessed value.

    More from the publisher:

    • Why are applicant signals missing on some jobs?
    • Why does jobUrl sometimes fail a curl/HEAD check?

    HTTP 401 means the token is missing; an empty file usually means the input matched nothing, which the run log in Console names.
