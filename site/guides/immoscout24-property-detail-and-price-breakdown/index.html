    ImmoScout24 Property Detail & Price Breakdown Scraper export

    Extract ImmoScout24 property listings for sale or rent in Germany: price (with cold rent/warm rent/utilities breakdown where listed), living space, rooms, address, coordinates, amenities, agent contact and images.

    This tutorial runs ImmoScout24 Property Detail & Price Breakdown Scraper through the Apify API and saves what it returns as a CSV file, an Excel workbook or JSON, keeping the 8 columns its publisher puts first: title, price, priceType, livingSpace, rooms, city, url and id.
