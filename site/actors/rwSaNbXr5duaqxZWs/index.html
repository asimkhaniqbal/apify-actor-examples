TikTok DSA Ads Transparency Library Scraper

Search TikTok's EU/EEA ad transparency database by keyword, advertiser or ad ID. Get creative media, advertiser identity, impression buckets, audit status and per-region/age/gender targeting.

Open on ApifyGitHub source

Input schema

Input fields, types, required flags, descriptions, defaults, and prefills come from the published definition. Prefill examples are distinct from defaults.

FieldTypeRequiredDescription
modestringoptional`library` = TikTok DSA Commercial Content Library (EU/EEA/UK/CH/TR). `creativeCenter` = global Top Ads inspiration feed. `both` = run both and label every row with `source`.
keywordstringoptionalSearch ads by keyword or advertiser name. Leave empty to browse recent ads in the selected region. (mode=library)
advertiserNamestringoptionalExact advertiser name as shown in the library, e.g. `NIKE Retail B.V.`. If the exact search finds nothing, the actor retries it as a keyword. (mode=library)

FieldTypeRequiredDescription
modestringoptional`library` = TikTok DSA Commercial Content Library (EU/EEA/UK/CH/TR). `creativeCenter` = global Top Ads inspiration feed. `both` = run both and label every row with `source`.
keywordstringoptionalSearch ads by keyword or advertiser name. Leave empty to browse recent ads in the selected region. (mode=library)
advertiserNamestringoptionalExact advertiser name as shown in the library, e.g. `NIKE Retail B.V.`. If the exact search finds nothing, the actor retries it as a keyword. (mode=library)
advertiserIdstringoptionalOptional TikTok business ID (`adv_biz_ids`) to disambiguate an advertiser search. Use it with the exact advertiser name. (mode=library)
adIdsarrayoptionalDirect list of TikTok ad IDs. Skips search and opens each ad's detail page. (mode=library)
startUrlsarrayoptionalPaste TikTok library search or ad-detail URLs. Search URLs carry their own region, keyword/advertiser, dates and filters; detail URLs are read as ad IDs. (mode=library)
regionstringoptionalTikTok DSA library covers EU/EEA countries plus the UK, Switzerland and Turkey. `all` searches the union (capped by TikTok), so split regions for deeper runs. (mode=library)
startDatestringoptionalEarliest last-shown date (`YYYY-MM-DD`). Default: one year ago. (mode=library)
endDatestringoptionalLatest last-shown date (`YYYY-MM-DD`). Default: today. (mode=library)
sortBystringoptionalResult order. (mode=library)
adTypestringoptionalFilter by creative type. (mode=library)
adStatusstringoptionalActive ads are still being shown; inactive ads have stopped. (mode=library)
agearrayoptionalOnly ads targeted at any of these age groups: `13-17`, `18-24`, `25-34`, `35-44`, `45-54`, `55+`. Leave empty for all ages. (mode=library)
genderstringoptionalOnly ads targeted at this gender. (mode=library)
uniqueReachstringoptionalFilter by TikTok's unique-users-seen band. (mode=library)
exactPhrasebooleanoptionalTreat the keyword as an exact phrase instead of individual words. (mode=library)
includeDetailsbooleanoptionalOpen each ad's detail page for caption, CTA, landing page, advertiser identity and age/gender reach breakdown. Slower; recommended for smaller runs. (mode=library)
countOnlybooleanoptionalReturn one summary row with the number of matching ads instead of collecting ads. (mode=library)
maxItemsintegeroptionalMaximum records to collect per source (1–5,000). TikTok caps a single library search at about 5,000 results.
ccCountrystringoptionalCreative Center market. (mode=creativeCenter or both)
ccPeriodstringoptionalPerformance window for Creative Center. (mode=creativeCenter or both)
ccOrderBystringoptionalHow Creative Center ranks ads. (mode=creativeCenter or both)
ccObjectivestringoptionalCampaign objective filter. (mode=creativeCenter or both)
ccIndustrystringoptionalCreative Center industry filter. 258 industries are available; leave `all` to sweep without an industry. (mode=creativeCenter or both)
ccLanguagestringoptionalAd language filter. (mode=creativeCenter or both)
ccSweepbooleanoptionalAnonymous visitors see only 20 ads per filter combination. Sweeping periods, rankings, objectives and formats collects many more unique ads. (mode=creativeCenter or both)
ccIncludeDetailsbooleanoptionalFetch each ad's landing page, comment and share counts. (mode=creativeCenter or both)

TikTok DSA Ads Transparency Library Scraper

Search TikTok's DSA ad transparency library across 33 European markets and the global Creative Center Top Ads from one actor. Get ad creatives, advertiser identity, first/last shown dates, unique-user reach, age/gender targeting, CTAs, landing pages and Creative Center performance signals — with no TikTok account, cookies, API key or proxy setup.

What this actor does

  • DSA library search by keyword, exact advertiser name (optionally with its business ID), ad ID or a pasted library URL — in 33 EU/EEA/UK/CH/TR markets or all of them.
  • Server-side filters for ad type, active/inactive status, age, gender, unique reach and six sort orders, plus an exact-phrase option.

FAQ

Do I need a TikTok account or cookies? No. Both sources are public. The actor opens TikTok's public pages once to establish an anonymous session; no login, cookie file or API key is involved.

How many DSA ads can one search return? TikTok serves 12 ads per page and caps a single search at roughly 5,000 results. Split by country, date range or keyword to go deeper. The actor warns when it reaches the cap.

Why are some ads missing details? TikTok marks some ads as unavailable or shows no detail data. Those rows keep their listing data and carry detailUnavailable: true. Detail pages are opened for at most 500 DSA ads and 200 Creative Center ads per run; rows beyond those limits keep their listing data.

Why are my video URLs sometimes expired? TikTok's media URLs are signed and time-limited. The actor converts the library's wrapper URLs into working CDN URLs; download or re-host the files promptly if you need them long-term.

What is the difference between the DSA library and Creative Center? The DSA library is the EU transparency database: dates, reach, targeting and advertiser identity. Creative Center is a global showcase of high-performing ads with CTR/likes/video renditions, but no dates or targeting.

Why does Creative Center return only 20 ads? Anonymous visitors get one page of 20 per filter combination. With ccSweep on, the actor sweeps periods, rankings, objectives and formats, deduplicates, and collects many more unique ads.

Can I search Creative Center by keyword? Not reliably without a TikTok login. The actor does not offer a Creative Center keyword field; it sweeps the public Top Ads surface instead.

How do exact advertiser searches work? TikTok matches the exact advertiser name (for example NIKE Retail B.V.). If that returns nothing, the actor retries the name as a keyword so you still get useful results.

Why do some fields stay empty? TikTok does not disclose them for every ad — for example spend, exact impressions, cities or device targeting. Empty fields are omitted rather than filled with placeholders.

Is this affiliated with TikTok? No. This is an independent third-party actor that reads TikTok's public ad transparency library and Creative Center pages.
