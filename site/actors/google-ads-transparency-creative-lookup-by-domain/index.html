    Google Ads Transparency Creative Lookup by Domain is an Apify Actor published by Vexaloom Labs. It accepts 10 input fields, and mode is required. Each result can carry up to 13 declared fields, including advertiserName, format, firstShownAt, lastShownAt, daysShown and destinationDomain. It costs $0.99 per 1,000 results on Apify's Free plan and $0.49 on the Business plan.

    What it does

    Research the ads a company runs through Google without a Google Ads account. Search the public Ads Transparency Center by landing-page domain, brand name, exact advertiser ID, or pasted URL, and export creatives with run dates, destination domains, variants, per-country activity, EU impression ranges, audience targeting, and verified advertiser identity. No login, no API key, no cookies.

    What this actor does

    • Search by domain, brand, advertiser ID, or URL in one batch — paste nike.com, Nike, AR16735076323512287233, or a Transparency Center link
    • Full result sets, not the first page — follows Google's continuation cursor to the end of the result set or your chosen limit
    • Ad details on demand — every creative variation, per-country run dates, EU impression ranges by country and platform, audience-selection categories, and advertiser country, legal name (when published), and verification
    • Ad-check mode — turn a domain or brand into one row per advertiser found, with verification status and, for exact advertiser-ID lookups, Google's approximate ad-count range
    • Filters: 249 countries plus Anywhere, text/image/video, Search/Maps/Shopping/Play/YouTube, and a shown-during date window
    • Empty fields are omitted — no nulls or placeholder strings

    Use cases

    • Competitive creative research — collect a swipe file of live and recent ads, with run dates and destination pages
    • Ad-check and lead generation — find which advertisers send clicks to a domain, with legal names and verification badges
    • EU reach analysis — compare impression ranges by country and platform for DSA-covered ads
    • Campaign longevity — use daysShown and lastShownAt to separate long-running winners from short tests
    • Brand safety and compliance — verify who is paying for ads that point at your domain
    • Market expansion — compare a brand's creative set across countries

    Questions

    Is this the official Google Ads API?

    No. It reads the public Ads Transparency Center, the same data any visitor can browse, without a Google Ads account, developer project, or API key.

    Do I need to paste a domain, a brand name, or an advertiser ID?

    Any of the three works. Brand names are resolved to the brand's website first because Google's advertiser-name lookup often leads with small regional accounts that share the name. Use an exact AR... ID when you need one specific account.

    Why does one domain return ads from several advertisers?

    A domain search returns every ad that points at that domain, including partners, resellers, and agencies. Use mode=advertisers to see the accounts involved, or search an AR... ID to isolate one account.

    Why are headline, description, or CTA missing on some ads?

    Google stores many search text ads only as a picture of the ad. The actor returns readable copy when Google exposes it, and omits the field when the creative has no machine-readable text.

    Why are impressions missing for some ads?

    Google publishes impression ranges for some ads shown in the EU/EEA and Turkey, after a delay. Coverage is partial and varies by market and ad: some ads have run dates but no impression figures even after three months. Per-country figures are in regions[]; the top-level totals cover the EU/EEA only.

    What does advertiserVerified mean?

    It reflects Google's advertiser identity verification badge and is returned whenever the advertiser lookup succeeds. false means Google shows the advertiser as not verified.

    Why is destinationUrl sometimes missing?

    Google does not expose a landing URL on every creative. destinationDomain comes from the search result when you search by domain; the full URL is added when the creative preview contains it.

    Can I search all countries at once?

    Yes — set region to ANYWHERE. Per-ad regions[] then shows where each creative ran, with EU impression data where Google publishes it.

    Does the actor include age-restricted ads?

    No. Google requires a signed-in account to show age-restricted creatives, and this actor never uses cookies or logins.

    Is this affiliated with Google?

    No. It is an independent tool that reads public transparency data and is not endorsed by Google.
