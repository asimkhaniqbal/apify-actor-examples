Yandex News Story Clusters Across Russia & the CIS

Yandex News groups coverage of the same event from many outlets into a story cluster. This actor reads the public Dzen News surface that now serves Yandex News, returns the clusters, and lists every linked article inside each one. Search by keyword, browse a category, read the homepage sections, or paste a story URL. No login, API key, or proxy is needed.

What this actor does

  • Four ways to collect news: keyword search, 16 category feeds, the 15 homepage sections, or specific story URLs and cluster IDs.
  • Story clusters, not just headlines: each story carries its cluster ID, source-reported coverage count, actual article count, unique source list, topics, image, country/region and timestamps.
  • Every linked article: article records include the publisher, headline, clean article URL, publication date, image and source flags.
  • Filters and sorting on the returned set: date window, source domain, publisher, keyword, relevance, newest, oldest or most covered.
  • Automatic deduplication by story ID and article ID, and empty fields are omitted rather than stored as nulls.

Use cases

  • Media monitoring: follow a company, person or event across many Russian outlets.
  • Share-of-voice measurement: use the reported and returned article counts to size coverage.
  • PR and communications: see which outlets picked up a story and how the headline evolved.
  • Newsroom research: export a cluster's full article list with dates and publisher links.
  • Risk and compliance: monitor keywords, source domains and publication windows.

FAQ

What happened to Yandex News? The public Yandex News pages now redirect to Dzen News, which provides the same story-cluster model. This actor reads that public surface.

How many stories can one search return? Yandex search returns 20 clusters per page and its depth varies by query; observed runs reached 9–22 pages (roughly 175–400 unique clusters) before the result set ended. maxStories is a hard run-wide cap.

Why is articleCount different from articlesReturned? articleCount is the number Yandex reports for the cluster, while articlesReturned is the number of article records actually collected. The two can differ in either direction: Yandex's live count changes as outlets publish or remove articles (observed 96 reported vs 122 returned, and 246 vs 362), and very large clusters may be capped by maxArticlesPerStory.

Are the date and sort filters applied by Yandex? Yandex's public search page applies them in the browser rather than in the server response. This actor applies the same filters and sorting to the set it returns, so a very old custom date range may return fewer results than a dedicated archive search.

Which categories are available? All, politics, society, SVO, business, world, sport, incidents, culture, technology, science, cars, army, show business, health, Moscow and interesting. all reads every feed.

Do I need a proxy or login? No. The actor connects directly and handles the site's anonymous session automatically. No cookies or credentials are required.

Can I search other regional editions or languages? The current public surface serves one Russian-language feed; the old ru/kz/by/uz/com edition selector no longer changes the content. Search queries can still be written in Russian, English or any language Yandex indexes.

Why is there no trending keyword list? The old “what people discuss” panel is no longer present on the homepage. topStories returns the homepage sections and their top clusters instead.

Does this actor download full article text? No. It returns article metadata and the publisher's URL. Full text stays on the publisher's site and may be paywalled or geo-restricted.

Is this affiliated with Yandex or Dzen? No. This is a third-party actor reading public pages; it is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Yandex or Dzen.
