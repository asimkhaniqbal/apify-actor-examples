    Global Sources Verified Supplier & Product Finder is an Apify Actor published by Vexaloom Labs. It accepts 5 input fields, and mode is required. Each result can carry up to 7 declared fields, including title, price, minOrder, supplier, supplierCountry and verifiedStatus. It costs $0.99 per 1,000 results on Apify's Free plan and $0.49 on the Business plan.

    What it does

    Global Sources is a B2B trade platform where buyers source manufacturers and products, mostly out of Asia, and where some supplier profiles carry an on-site "audited/verified" badge. This actor runs product keyword searches, supplier keyword searches, or category browsing, and can restrict any of the three to audited/verified suppliers only.

    What this actor does

    • Three modes: searchProducts (product keyword search), searchSuppliers (supplier keyword search), categoryBrowse (browse a fixed product category instead of a keyword)
    • Verified-only filter drops any product or supplier that doesn't carry a verified/audited badge
    • Paginates automatically through result pages until maxItems is reached or a page returns fewer than 5 results
    • Covers ten top-level categories for categoryBrowse — Electronics, Fashion & Accessories, Home & Garden, Auto & Transportation, Gifts & Premiums, Health & Beauty, Computer & Networking, Machinery & Industrial, Building & Construction, Telecommunications
    • Empty fields are omitted — a listing only has the fields Global Sources actually displayed for it

    Use cases

    • Sourcing shortlists — pull a first-pass list of manufacturers for a product you want to import
    • Verified-supplier vetting — filter straight to audited suppliers before reaching out
    • Category price scanning — browse a whole category to see the spread of prices and MOQs
    • Competitive intelligence — see which suppliers are listing a specific product keyword and at what price
    • Procurement research — compare minimum order quantities across multiple suppliers before negotiating

    Questions

    What does a "verified" or "audited" badge mean?

    Global Sources runs on-site factory audits for some suppliers and marks their listings accordingly; it's a trust signal the platform itself assigns, not something this actor calculates.

    Does verifiedOnly work in every mode?

    Yes — it applies to products, suppliers, and category browsing alike, dropping any record without a verification badge.

    Why do some products show a price range instead of one number?

    Global Sources often publishes a price range tied to order quantity tiers; the actor returns exactly what's shown on the listing rather than picking one number.

    Why are some supplier records missing products?

    That field only appears when the supplier's listing shows its product categories; not every profile publishes this.

    Can I browse a category and filter by keyword at the same time?

    No — as long as category is set, categoryBrowse ignores keyword and lists everything in that category; use searchProducts with a keyword if you need both a category feel and a specific term. If category is left blank ("All categories"), the run falls back to a keyword product search instead of browsing every category.

    Do I need a Global Sources account to run this?

    No — it reads only the public search, category, and listing pages that are visible without logging in.

    Is this affiliated with Global Sources?

    No, this is an independent third-party actor that reads Global Sources' public pages. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Global Sources Ltd.
