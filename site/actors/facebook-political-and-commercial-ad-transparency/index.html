    Facebook Political & Commercial Ad Transparency Extractor is an Apify Actor published by Vexaloom Labs. It accepts 21 input fields, none of them required at the top level. Each result can carry up to 55 declared fields, including ad_id, page_name, ad_text, start_date, status and media_type. It costs $0.99 per 1,000 results on Apify's Free plan and $0.49 on the Business plan.

    What it does

    Read Facebook's public Ad Library directly and export one clean JSON record per ad — creative copy, carousel cards, images and videos, placements, advertiser identity, run dates, and the transparency numbers Meta only publishes for political and EU-delivered ads (spend ranges, impressions, reach, payer/beneficiary, age/country/gender reach). Works with a plain guest session: no Facebook login, no cookies, no Meta developer app.

    Data source

    The actor talks to the same internal JSON endpoint the Ad Library web page uses (facebook.com/api/graphql/, guest session). The page's own query identifiers are re-learned from the live site when they rotate, so the actor keeps working across Meta releases. No credential of any kind is required. Requests run through a built-in connection ladder (direct first, an Apify datacenter pool, then residential as a last resort) chosen automatically per request — you never configure a proxy.

    The publisher's output notes

    What you get

    • Four ways to search — keywords, advertiser Pages, exact Ad Library IDs, or the funding entity on a "Paid for by" disclaimer, all in one run if you want
    • Meta's own transparency payloads, re-emitted whole — political spend and impression ranges; EU total reach plus the per-country age/gender reach breakdown; declared payer and beneficiary; audience age/gender/location distributions returned as Meta publishes them
    • Creative in full — primary media URL (copied to a permanent URL before it can expire), every image and video URL, carousel cards with their own text/CTA/link, CTA type, caption, title and display format
    • Advertiser context — Page URL, profile picture, likes, categories, verification, and the linked Instagram handle with follower count and About text
    • Scale signals — collation_count (variants sharing the same creative), days_running, placements, and the special-ad categories
    • Honest gaps — a field Meta does not disclose for an ad is simply left out; nothing is estimated or fabricated

    Questions

    Do I need a Facebook account or cookies?

    No. The actor works with an anonymous guest session by default.

    Why is spend missing on some ads?

    Meta publishes spend and impressions ranges only for political/issue ads and EU-regulated advertising. The actor returns those fields when the source has them and omits them otherwise — it never fills a gap with a guess.

    Why did my political search for an EU country return all ad types?

    Meta's political/issue archive does not cover EU/EEA countries; EU political ads live in the general transparency stream, so the actor automatically searches all types there and says so in the run log.

    Will image and video links keep working?

    Facebook's media links are signed and expire within days. With rehostMedia on, the primary media is copied to a permanent URL first and the original is preserved in media_url_original; the arrays keep Meta's URLs, each with its expiry in media_url_expires_at.

    How far back does the data go?

    Whatever the public Ad Library serves for your query: seven years for political/issue ads, one year for ads delivered to the EU, and live archive coverage for commercial ads.

    Can I scrape several countries or targets at once?

    Yes — countries yields a union, and every target list can run together in one job.

    What is collation_count?

    The number of ad variants Meta groups under the same creative and text — a quick way to spot heavily scaled campaigns.

    Why does ad_text sometimes contain {{product.brand}}?

    Dynamic-creative (DCO/DPA) ads are stored with template placeholders; Meta resolves them only when the ad is delivered. The actor keeps the served text untouched in ad_text, records the placeholder in template_body/template_tokens, and attaches resolved_ad_text/resolved_title when the payload's cards contain the resolved creative instead of inventing one.

    Is the total count exact?

    It is the number the Ad Library page itself displays for your filters. Meta caps the display at 50,001, meaning "50,000 or more"; below that it is exact. Totals are not available for byline searches because the Ad Library UI has no byline filter.
