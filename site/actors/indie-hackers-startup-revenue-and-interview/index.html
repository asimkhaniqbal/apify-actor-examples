    Indie Hackers Startup Revenue & Interview Scraper is an Apify Actor published by Vexaloom Labs. It accepts 24 input fields, and mode is required. Each result can carry up to 12 declared fields, including recordType, name, title, tagline, monthlyRevenue and revenueVerified. It costs $0.99 per 1,000 results on Apify's Free plan and $0.49 on the Business plan.

    What it does

    Indie Hackers is a public directory of bootstrapped products and the founders behind them. This actor reads the cookieless HTML the site serves today and returns product records with revenue, month-by-month revenue history, founders, screenshots, built-with tools and related products, plus the filterable founder case-study database.

    No account, cookie, API key or proxy configuration is required. The site's public pages are fetched with a browser-compatible TLS profile, and every field comes from what the page actually shows.

    Modes

    Mode What it returns
    products Product records from the product database, including monthly revenue and optional Stripe revenue history. Supports product keyword, category, business model, audience, revenue range and Stripe-verified filters, sorting, and pagination.
    interviews Founder case studies from the case-study database. Supports keyword, industry, learnings, business-model, revenue, employee-count and location filters, four sort orders, and pagination.
    byUrl Resolves exact /product/<slug> and /post/<id> URLs (old /interview/<id> links are mapped too).

    Questions

    Do I need cookies or a proxy?

    No. The actor reads the public pages with a browser TLS fingerprint and no user credentials. A proxy is not required or used.

    Why can I only see 18 products without an account?

    That is the site's own guest limit. The actor keeps discovering products through the related-product graph on every product page and, when Cloudflare allows it for the run's IP, through the public product sitemap. byUrl resolves any product URL directly, even one outside the discovered set.

    Why are some revenue fields empty?

    Many founders never publish revenue. Revenue above 10 trillion USD per month, zero and negative values are treated as invalid and omitted rather than emitted as fake data.

    Where does revenue history come from?

    From the Stripe-backed chart embedded in the product page. Products that only report a single self-reported figure get monthlyRevenue and revenueLabel, not a fabricated history.

    Are groups supported?

    No. Indie Hackers removed community groups; the old /group/ pages return HTTP 410. Older inputs are handled without crashing and return a clear status message.

    What about old input names?

    Saved tasks that still send foundedDate-desc or memberCount-desc as sortBy keep running: those values stay in the input schema and are mapped in code to the closest supported order. mode=groups is accepted, detected and explained in the run status. Legacy urls, pageUrl and query keys are mapped in code too.

    Why is the product order slightly different from the website?

    verified-revenue reproduces the site's "Revenue: Verified First" order. revenue-desc sorts by the numeric revenue, so self-reported products can rank above a lower verified figure.

    Can I page through results?

    Yes. Filter and sort first, then use offset with maxItems. For products the discovered pool is bounded (the guest directory plus related products and any accessible sitemap URLs), so a very large offset can return fewer rows.
