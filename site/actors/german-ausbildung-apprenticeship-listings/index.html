    German Ausbildung Apprenticeship Listings Extractor is an Apify Actor published by Vexaloom Labs. It accepts 18 input fields, and mode is required. Each result can carry up to 7 declared fields, including title, professionTitle, corporationName, location, startsNoEarlierThan and vacancyCount. It costs $0.99 per 1,000 results on Apify's Free plan and $0.49 on the Business plan.

    Input reference

    All 18 fields of the published input schema, in the publisher's order. Required fields are marked. Download the input schema as JSON.

    Input fields in the published schema
    FieldTypeDescription
    modeModestring requiredWhat to fetch. byUrl reads the URLs in searchUrls.Default "byCity"; Allowed byCity, byProfession, byQuery, byUrl
    searchUrlsSearch URLs (any mode)arrayPaste one or more ausbildung.de search URLs (city+category, profession, or keyword search). When set, these URLs override mode and the single-value location inputs. Examples: https://www.ausbildung.de/staedte/berlin/it/ , https://www.ausbildung.de/suche/?search=Fachinformatiker , https://www.ausbildung.de/suche/?professions=Fachinformatiker%2Fin
    citySlugCity (mode=byCity)stringGerman city name/slug, e.g. berlin, muenchen, hamburg, koeln. Umlauts should be transliterated (ä→ae, ö→oe, ü→ue, ß→ss) and spaces become hyphens — the actor also normalizes common input automatically.Default "berlin"
    categoryCategory (mode=byCity)stringBroad topic/industry to filter the city search. kaufmaennisch (commercial/office) is the broadest category and is populated for virtually every city. Not every category has listings in every city — an unlisted combination returns 0 records, not an error. Note: alle-berufe (unfiltered) is intentionally excluded because ausbildung.de renders it as an infinite-scroll page with no listings in the initial server response.Default "kaufmaennisch"; Allowed kaufmaennisch, abitur, autos, drogistin, elektronik, fachkraft-fuer-lagerlogistik, fachmann-systemgastronomie, fachverkaeufer-lebensmittelhandwerk and 22 more
    radiusKmSearch radius around the city (km)stringOnly used in city mode. Round the search around the selected city. 30 km is the site default; 200 km covers the wider metro area.Default "30"; Allowed 5, 10, 20, 25, 30, 50, 75, 100 and 1 more
    sortBySort results bystringServer-side result ordering. Relevance is the site default; Aktualität shows recently activated listings first.Default "relevance"; Allowed relevance, start_date, distance, activated_at
    professionSlugProfession slug (mode=byProfession)stringExact ausbildung.de profession slug, nationwide search (no city filter). Examples: fachinformatiker, kauffrau-im-einzelhandel, mechatroniker, altenpfleger, bankkaufmann, koch, elektroniker-betriebstechnik. Browse the full glossary at ausbildung.de/berufe/glossar/ for all ~700 slugs.Default "fachinformatiker"
    searchQuerySearch query (mode=byQuery)stringFree-text keyword searched nationwide across profession titles and company names, e.g. Fachinformatiker, Bäcker, McDonald's. Broader than professionSlug (exact slug match) — matches partial/fuzzy profession and employer names.Default "Fachinformatiker"
    expectedGraduationRequired school diplomastringFilter results to listings requiring this school-leaving qualification. Mapped to ausbildung.de's own filter values server-side and re-checked on the records.Default ""; Allowed , hauptschulabschluss, qualifizierter-hauptschulabschluss, mittlerer-schulabschluss, fachabitur, abitur, ohne-schulabschluss, keine-angabe
    apprenticeshipTypeApprenticeship typestringFilter by the type of vocational training program.Default ""; Allowed , klassische-duale-berufsausbildung, schulische-ausbildung, schulische-und-duale-ausbildung, duales-studium, abiturientenprogramm, betriebsinterne-ausbildung, weiterbildung
    startDateFromTraining starts on or after (YYYY-MM-DD)stringOnly keep listings whose training start date (startsNoEarlierThan) is on or after this date.
    startDateToTraining starts on or before (YYYY-MM-DD)stringOnly keep listings whose training start date (startsNoEarlierThan) is on or before this date.
