    JobStreet Southeast Asia Job Search by Keyword & Country is an Apify Actor published by Vexaloom Labs. It accepts 41 input fields, and mode is required. Each result can carry up to 15 declared fields, including recordType, jobId, title, name, company and location. It costs $0.99 per 1,000 results on Apify's Free plan and $0.49 on the Business plan.

    What it does

    Scrape JobStreet and JobsDB across six Southeast Asian markets — Malaysia, Singapore, Indonesia, the Philippines, Thailand and Hong Kong. Get full job ads with parsed salary ranges and applicant demand, company profiles with employee reviews, and role salary benchmarks, all without a login.

    What this actor does

    • Five modes: keyword search, scrape job / search / company URLs, company jobs, company profiles, and role salary benchmarks.
    • Full job ads: description text or HTML, bullet points, screening questions, work type, work arrangement, classification, expiry date and apply link.
    • Structured salaries: parsed minimum, maximum, currency and period from the posted salary label, plus a strict salary filter.
    • Applicant demand: applicant count, application volume and resume / cover-letter percentages when the employer publishes them.
    • Company intelligence: rating, review count, industry, size, website, perks, employee reviews, open jobs and salary ranges by role.
    • Incremental runs: emit only new or changed jobs between scheduled runs, with NEW / UPDATED / REAPPEARED / EXPIRED change types.
    • Clean output: fields the source does not show are omitted; false and 0 are kept.

    Services (modes)

    Mode What it returns
    searchJobs Job records from one or more keywords and locations, with server-side category, work type, arrangement, date and sort filters plus strict client-side salary, keyword and applicant filters.
    byUrls Job records from job URLs, job records from search URLs, and company records from company URLs. Each URL's own market is used.
    companyJobs Open job records for one or more companies, found by company URL, numeric id or name.
    companyProfiles One company record per company, with optional reviews, salary ranges by role and current open jobs.
    salaryBenchmarks One record per role with typical monthly salary, growth, job satisfaction and classification, plus optional role details and state / city salary breakdowns.

    How it works

    The actor reads the same public listings the JobStreet and JobsDB sites show, using each market's own host, language and currency. Full job descriptions, company profiles, reviews and salary benchmarks come from the sites' public listing data rather than rendered pages, so results are consistent and fast. No account, cookie or proxy setup is required. Vietnam is not included because JobStreet Vietnam now redirects to Jora; Thailand is served by JobsDB at th.jobsdb.com.

    Use cases

    • Recruitment market research: compare demand, salaries and work arrangements across six Southeast Asian markets.
    • Compensation benchmarking: collect posted salary ranges and official role salary guides in local currencies.
    • Job feed aggregation: power a regional job board with full descriptions, expiry dates and apply links.
    • Talent sourcing: find companies hiring for a role and pull their profile, reviews and open jobs.
    • Competitive intelligence: track which employers are hiring, how often roles are refreshed and what they pay.
    • AI and MCP pipelines: use compact output for lightweight, structured job feeds.

    Questions

    Which countries are covered?

    Malaysia, Singapore, Indonesia, the Philippines (JobStreet) plus Thailand and Hong Kong (JobsDB). Vietnam is no longer a JobStreet market and redirects to Jora, so it is not offered.

    Do I need a JobStreet account or cookies?

    No. The actor reads public listings and public company data without a login, cookie or proxy.

    Why is salary missing on some jobs?

    Many employers do not publish a salary. When the posted label contains no real salary — for example "Weekends Off, Remote" — the salary fields are omitted rather than guessed.

    How does the salary filter work when the site's own filter is inexact?

    The actor parses each job's salary and keeps only jobs whose advertised range overlaps your minimum and maximum. Jobs without a published salary are excluded when a salary bound is set. Salary values are converted between monthly, annual and hourly for the comparison.

    Can one run search several markets?

    Each run uses one country, or the market of each URL in byUrls. Run once per market if you need a combined dataset.

    How complete are company reviews and salary ranges?

    They are returned when the employer has claimed a company profile and has published reviews or salary data. Jobs without a profile still return the job fields; the company enrichment is simply omitted.

    What is the difference between companyJobs and companyProfiles?

    companyJobs returns individual open jobs for the companies you supply. companyProfiles returns one record per company with optional reviews, salary ranges and a list of open jobs.

    How does incremental mode decide a job changed?

    It compares title, company, location, salary, description, applicant count and expiry between runs. New jobs are NEW, changed jobs UPDATED, returning jobs REAPPEARED, and disappeared jobs EXPIRED when enabled. Around 50,000 job ids are remembered per state key.

    Is this affiliated with JobStreet or JobsDB?

    No. This actor reads public listings and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by JobStreet, JobsDB or SEEK.

    Limits and notes

    Notes

    • Empty fields are omitted at every level; false and 0 are preserved.
    • Dates are ISO 8601 in UTC; postedDate is a YYYY-MM-DD date.
    • Salary amounts are emitted in the market's currency unless the label names another currency.
    • Company URLs and job URLs always point at the canonical market host.
    • Private-advertiser jobs keep the site's "Private Advertiser" company label; company profile fields are omitted when no profile exists.
    • byUrls accepts job links, search links and company links, including legacy JobStreet and JobsDB domains; a page number in a search link is honoured.
    • A company list with several companies is interleaved, so maxItems covers every company instead of stopping at the first one.
    • The default daily run is one small Singapore search with three jobs and full details.

    Limitations

    • Company coverage is conditional. Not every employer has a claimed profile, rating, reviews or salary ranges. Fields are omitted when absent.
    • Salary labels vary. The parser handles ranges, single figures, k, "Up to", monthly/annual/hourly periods, and Malaysian, Singaporean, Indonesian, Philippine, Thai and Hong Kong formats. Unusual or malformed labels may be omitted.
    • Search results change constantly. The same query can return a different order or new jobs between runs; use incremental mode and a stable stateKey for scheduled monitoring.
    • Listing depth follows the site's own result count. Pages are 100 jobs each and stop at the source's total; very broad queries can return many pages.
    • No HTML fallback. The actor relies on the public listing data the sites expose; if a market changes that data, the affected fields are omitted and the run status reports the failure.
    • No built-in notifications. Use Apify schedules and integrations to act on new or changed jobs.
    • Review data is employer-published. Reviews can be old, sparse or absent for smaller companies.
