    Amazon Customer Review & Rating Summary Scraper is an Apify Actor published by Vexaloom Labs. It accepts 18 input fields, none of them required at the top level. Each result can carry up to 28 declared fields, including productAsin, ratingScore, reviewTitle, reviewDescription, isVerified and reviewedIn. It costs $0.99 per 1,000 results on Apify's Free plan and $0.49 on the Business plan.

    What it costs

    Amazon Customer Review & Rating Summary Scraper uses pay-per-event pricing: a fee for each result saved to the dataset, plus a start fee per run. Paid Apify plans pay less per result. The start fee is charged once per GB of run memory (at least once); the default run uses 4 GB, so a run starts with 4 start events, as the run totals include. You also pay Apify platform usage (compute and proxy) for the run, which these totals do not include.

    FieldTypeDescription
    productUrlsProduct URLs or ASINsarrayAmazon product URLs (/dp/, /gp/product/, /gp/aw/d/, /product/) or bare 10-character ASINs, on any supported Amazon domain. Leave empty to discover products from keywords/categoryUrls instead.Example ["https://www.amazon.com/dp/B09X7MPX8L"]
    keywordsKeywords (discover products)arraySearch terms used to discover products when productUrls is empty (e.g. "wireless earbuds"). Each keyword contributes up to maxProductsToDiscover products.Default []
    categoryUrlsCategory or Search URLs (discover products)arrayAdvanced discovery: Amazon /s?... search URLs or /b?node=... category URLs. Used only when productUrls is empty.Default []
    maxProductsToDiscoverMax Products to DiscoverintegerMaximum products discovered from each keyword or category URL (1-50). Ignored when productUrls is supplied.Default 5; Allowed 1 to 50
    countryAmazon MarketplacestringAmazon storefront to use for bare ASINs, discovery, and products whose URL does not carry a domain. Product URLs keep their own domain.Default "amazon.com"; Allowed amazon.com, amazon.co.uk, amazon.de, amazon.fr, amazon.it, amazon.es, amazon.ca, amazon.com.au and 15 more
    maxReviewsMax Reviews per ProductintegerHard cap per product after merging and deduplication (1-2000). The product-page sample alone yields about 8-13 rows; the optional extended feed can add 100-800+ for popular US products. In optional cookie mode this is the paginated cap.Default 100; Example 15; Allowed 1 to 2000
    extendedHistoryExtended Review HistorybooleanMerge Amazon's public extended review feed for each ASIN (no login): up to 100 rows per star/sort bucket, typically 100-800+ unique reviews for popular US products, deduplicated against the product-page sample. Coverage varies by ASIN and the feed carries Amazon.com reviews only.Default true
    mergeMarketplacesMerge Extra MarketplacesarrayFetch the same product on these additional Amazon marketplaces and merge their deduplicated product-page reviews. Empty = the product's own marketplace only.Default []; Allowed amazon.com, amazon.co.uk, amazon.de, amazon.fr, amazon.it, amazon.es, amazon.ca, amazon.com.au and 15 more
    sortBySort Reviews BystringOrder of the returned reviews: most helpful first, or most recent first.Default "helpful"; Allowed helpful, recent
    filterByStarFilter by Star RatingstringKeep only reviews with this rating. positive keeps 4-5 stars and critical keeps 1-2 stars. Applied after merging, so narrow filters can legitimately return few or no rows.Default ""; Allowed , 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, positive, critical
    verifiedPurchasesOnlyVerified Purchases OnlybooleanKeep only reviews carrying Amazon's Verified Purchase badge. Applied server-side in the extended feed (and cookie mode) and client-side for product-page rows.Default false
    filterKeywordsFilter by KeywordsarrayKeep only reviews whose title or text contains at least one of these keywords (case-insensitive). Empty = no keyword filter.Default []
