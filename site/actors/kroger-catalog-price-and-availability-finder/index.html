    Kroger Catalog Price & Availability Finder is an Apify Actor published by Vexaloom Labs. It accepts 7 input fields, and mode is required. Each result can carry up to 6 declared fields, including title, brandName, price, size, upc and productUrl. It costs $0.99 per 1,000 results on Apify's Free plan and $0.49 on the Business plan.

    What it does

    Kroger.com runs the product catalog for America's largest grocery chain. This actor searches that catalog by keyword or looks up specific items by UPC/GTIN, returning product title, brand, size, regular and sale price, unit price, dietary information, department, aisle location, inventory level and product photos.

    What this actor does

    • Two modes: search (keyword catalog search) and byUpc (exact UPC/GTIN lookup)
    • Paginated search with 24 results per request and price filters applied to every record
    • Rich records: price, unit price, sale price, equivalized price, dietary info, department/commodity taxonomy, aisle, stock level
    • Product photos: front thumbnail and large image URLs built from the UPC
    • Empty fields are omitted

    Use cases

    • Price monitoring — track Kroger prices and sales over time
    • CPG analytics — product-level assortment, size and unit-price research
    • Dietary research — filter catalogs by vegetarian/kosher/organic claims
    • Competitive intelligence — benchmark Kroger pricing against other chains
    • Shopping apps — enrich products with aisle and stock data

    Questions

    What is the data source?

    Kroger's own public product search and catalog pages at kroger.com. This is an independent third-party actor — not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by The Kroger Co.

    Do I need to log in?

    No. The actor reads the same public product data the website shows to any visitor — no account, cookies or API keys required.

    Are the prices real-time?

    Yes — every run reflects current store prices, including sale prices and unit pricing.

    Why are some fields missing?

    Empty fields are omitted. Fields like salePrice only exist when a product is on sale.

    Why is productUrl a template?

    Kroger product URLs are built as kroger.com/p/<slug>/pid_<upc>. The actor doesn't have the slug text, so productUrl contains a literal {slug} placeholder in that position with the real UPC after pid_; the upc field lets you look the product up directly if that link doesn't resolve for you.

    When would I need the proxy configuration?

    Only if Kroger temporarily rate-limits requests from Apify's infrastructure — leave it on AUTO unless you see repeated failures.

    Can I search in other languages?

    Search is keyword-based and matches the English catalog.
