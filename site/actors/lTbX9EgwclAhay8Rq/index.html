Published README

Read the full published documentation

Ozon.ru Product Pricing, Ratings & Seller Scraper

Scrape Ozon — one of Russia's largest marketplaces — for products, prices, sellers, reviews, and buyer questions. Five modes cover keyword search, category browsing, product lookup by URL or SKU, seller storefronts, and review/Q&A collection. Prices follow the selected storefront (RUB, BYN, or KZT), and every record is ready for price monitoring, assortment analysis, seller vetting, and catalog enrichment.

What this actor does

  • Five modes: search, byCategory, productDetails, seller, reviews
  • Three storefronts: Ozon Russia (RUB), Belarus (BYN), Kazakhstan (KZT)
  • Product depth: description, full specification table, package contents, variants, gallery, videos, hashtags, rating distribution, and availability
  • Seller intelligence: legal entity, registration number, address, years on Ozon, premium status, confirmed brands, catalog products, and competing buybox offers
  • Reviews and Q&A: review text, rating, author, date, photos, videos, helpful votes, verified-purchase flag, seller replies, plus buyer questions with answers
  • Filters and sorts: price range, minimum rating, in-stock only, and six listing sort orders
  • Empty fields are omitted — records contain only data that was actually found

Services

  • Product search and category scraping — collect listing rows with live price, discount, rating, review count, image, stock, and delivery estimate.
  • Full product cards — enrich any product with specifications, description, variants, seller details, and the complete buybox.
  • Seller monitoring — read a seller's public profile, legal identity, and catalog in one run.
  • Review and Q&A collection — build voice-of-customer datasets with review media and seller responses.
  • Cross-market pricing — compare the same marketplace across Russia, Belarus, and Kazakhstan in local currency.

How it works

Ozon publishes its storefront data as a public widget tree. The actor reads that public data through a translation mirror, so no Ozon account, cookie, login, API key, or proxy setup is required. Listings are followed with Ozon's own pagination tokens, not hand-built page numbers. Product details combine the main product page, the description/specification page, and the reviews page. Reviews are collected 30 rows per page and buyer questions 10 rows per page, following the site's own next-page tokens until maxReviewsPerProduct (reviews) or maxItems (questions) is reached. Duplicate products and duplicate review/question rows are removed by their natural identifiers.

Output

One dataset holds four record types, distinguished by recordType. Actor views provide product, seller, review, and question table layouts; filter on recordType to isolate one type.

Product rows (recordType: "product")

  • sku, name, productUrl, imageUrl, images[], videos[]
  • brand — only when Ozon publishes an explicit brand value
  • price, originalPrice, discountPercent, cardPrice, currency, storefront
  • rating, reviewCount, ratingBreakdown[] — star buckets with count and percentage
  • sellerName, sellerUrl, sellerRating, sellerOrdersCount
  • sellerId, sellerLegalName, sellerRegistrationNumber, sellerAddress, sellerYearsOnOzon, sellerIsPremium, sellerConfirmedBrands[] (when seller details are enabled)
  • otherSellers[], otherSellersCount, otherSellersMinPrice (when buybox is enabled)
  • category, breadcrumbs[]
  • characteristics — full specification table when available, otherwise the short product panel
  • characteristicsCount, description, packageContents, hashtags[]
  • variants[] — other colors/configurations with their own SKU, price, URL, and availability
  • questionCount, similarProducts[], marketingLabels[]
  • availability, availabilityText, unitsLeft, stockQuantity, deliveryText, deliveryInfo
  • recordType, scrapedAt

Seller rows (recordType: "seller")

  • sellerId, name, url, logoUrl, storefront
  • rating, reviewsCount, ordersCount, yearsOnOzon, isPremium
  • legalName, registrationNumber, address, operatingHours, confirmedBrands[]
  • catalogProducts, scrapedAt

Review rows (recordType: "review")

  • reviewId, sku, productUrl
  • rating, authorName, publishedAt
  • comment, pros, cons (pros/cons are usually absent in current Ozon reviews)
  • photos[], videos[]
  • usefulCount, notUsefulCount, isPurchaseVerified
  • commentsCount, sellerResponse, badgeDelivery
  • recordType, scrapedAt

Question rows (recordType: "question")

  • questionId, sku, productUrl
  • text, authorName, createdAt, likes, dislikes
  • answers[] — each with authorName, createdAt, text, likes, dislikes, and isBestAnswer
  • recordType, scrapedAt

Use cases

  • Price monitoring — track product, card, and original prices across three Ozon storefronts.
  • Market and assortment research — compare categories, brands, ratings, and discount depth.
  • Seller vetting — collect legal identity, order history, and confirmed brands before partnering.
  • Competitive intelligence — see which sellers hold the buybox and at what delivered price.
  • Voice of customer — export review text, media, and buyer questions for product teams.
  • Catalog enrichment — fill your own listings with structured specifications and variants.

Usage cost

This actor uses standard Apify platform usage and does not add per-event charges. A typical search run reads one listing page per batch of results; enabling product details, buybox, seller details, or reviews adds one or more page reads per product. Keep maxItems and maxReviewsPerProduct close to what you need.

FAQ

Do I need an Ozon account, cookies, or a proxy? No. The actor reads Ozon's public storefront data without credentials, and you do not configure any proxy.

What is the difference between price and cardPrice? price is the regular selling price and cardPrice is the lower Ozon Card price when the product page shows one.

Why is brand sometimes missing? Ozon often shows a trust badge such as "Оригинал" instead of a real brand name. The actor only emits brand when Ozon publishes an explicit brand value and never guesses it from the title.

Why is deliveryInfo often missing while deliveryText appears? Product pages frequently carry an empty delivery widget. deliveryText comes from a visible listing or buybox estimate; deliveryInfo only appears when the product page itself publishes delivery text.

Why are pros and cons usually absent? Current Ozon reviews usually expose a single comment body and leave the separate positive/negative fields empty. The actor includes them when they exist but does not split comments itself.

What does maxItems count in seller mode? It counts every emitted record, including the seller profile row. With maxItems: 20, you get the seller row plus up to 19 catalog products.

Can I collect every review and question for a product? Reviews are capped by maxReviewsPerProduct and read 30 per page. Questions are read 10 per page and capped by maxItems, not by maxReviewsPerProduct. Both follow Ozon's next-page token, so large histories are limited by your input rather than a fixed page count.

Are the three storefronts the same catalog? They share many listings but not all. Prices and availability differ, and the currency follows the storefront you choose.

Why do deep results under a non-default sort sometimes look less relevant? Ozon itself appends recommendation filler after a narrow sorted feed runs out. The actor returns what the site returns; for strict topical relevance, use the default relevance sort.

Is this affiliated with Ozon? No. This is an independent third-party actor that reads public storefront data and is not operated or endorsed by Ozon.

Notes

  • Empty values are omitted at every depth; there are no null fields.
  • Product, review, and question rows are deduplicated by their natural identifiers.
  • publishedAt on reviews is an ISO 8601 UTC timestamp; question createdAt keeps Ozon's displayed date text.
  • Partial failures do not stop the run: valid rows are still returned, and the run status message reports how many targets failed.
  • Legacy task inputs (searchQuery, categoryUrl, -price, and proxyConfiguration) are accepted in code; proxyConfiguration is ignored.

Limitations

  • Public data only. Private account data, cart contents, orders, and checkout pricing are not available.
  • Mirror dependency. Ozon's public pages are read through a translation mirror. If the mirror rate-limits or is unavailable, the run returns partial data and reports it in the status message.
  • Brand, delivery, and similar-product shelves are published inconsistently by Ozon. They are included only when the page carries them.
  • Search relevance filler. Ozon may append recommendations on deep sorted pages; the actor does not claim stricter relevance than the source.
  • Question dates are kept exactly as Ozon displays them, which may be a localized date rather than an ISO timestamp.
  • Seller catalogs reflect the public storefront at run time; hidden or regional-only listings may not appear.
