    Guru Freelance Jobs Scraper is an Apify Actor published by Vexaloom Labs. It accepts 9 input fields, and mode is required. Each result can carry up to 6 declared fields, including title, category, budgetType, employerCountry, postedAt and jobUrl. It costs $0.99 per 1,000 results on Apify's Free plan and $0.49 on the Business plan.

    What it does

    Scrape live freelance job postings from Guru.com — one of the largest freelance marketplaces. Browse jobs by category, keyword, and budget type, or pull full job detail pages (description, skills, employer stats) from a list of job URLs. No login, no API key, no cookies required.

    What this actor does

    • Two modes: browse (category listing) and byJobId (full job detail pages)
    • 7 top-level categories covering the whole marketplace: Programming & Development, Design & Art, Writing & Translation, Sales & Marketing, Administrative & Secretarial, Education & Training, Engineering & Architecture
    • Optional subcategory drill-down (e.g. "Web Development & Design")
    • Keyword filter — best-effort client-side match on title/description/skills
    • Budget-type filter — Fixed Price / Hourly / Fixed Price or Hourly
    • Rich per-job data: skills, budget range, hours/week, project duration, location preference, employer country, quotes received, application deadline
    • Detail mode adds full job description, employer payment-verification status, feedback score, total spend, and jobs-posted history
    • Automatically engages the free Apify AUTO datacenter proxy if Guru.com temporarily rate-limits direct requests
    • Empty fields are always omitted — every field in the output is backed by real scraped data

    Use cases

    • Freelance market research — track how many jobs post per category and at what budget type
    • Lead generation for freelancers — pull fresh postings in a skill category to find new clients
    • Rate benchmarking — compare budgetRange and budgetType across jobs in the same subcategory
    • Employer due diligence — pull full detail pages to check an employer's payment-verification status and posting history before bidding
    • Category trend tracking — run category=all on a schedule to see which categories are most active over time

    Questions

    Do I need a Guru.com account?

    No. All data comes from public listing and job-detail pages.

    Why did I get 0 results?

    Either the chosen category/subcategory combination has no active postings right now, or your keyword filter didn't match anything on the fetched pages — try a broader category or remove the keyword.

    Why is keyword described as "best-effort"?

    Guru.com's own search box is client-side only, so there's no server-side search endpoint to query. The actor instead fetches the category/subcategory listing pages and filters the fetched jobs by keyword locally, which means a keyword can only match jobs that were already within maxPages of listings.

    What's the difference between category and subcategory browsing?

    category is a true server-side filter on Guru.com's listing pages. subcategory narrows further within that category, but is matched against the subcategory text the actor already retrieved — pick the exact subcategory name shown on Guru.com for best results.

    Why do some jobs lack employerCountry or quotesReceived?

    These only appear when Guru.com shows them on the listing or detail page; some employers don't display location, and newly posted jobs may not yet show a quote count.

    When would I need the proxy configuration?

    Only if Guru.com temporarily rate-limits direct requests from Apify's infrastructure — the default free datacenter group is usually enough.

    Is this affiliated with Guru.com?

    No, this is an independent third-party actor that reads Guru.com's public job pages. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Guru.com, Inc.
