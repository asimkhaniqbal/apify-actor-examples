    Apartment Therapy Article Scraper is an Apify Actor published by Vexaloom Labs. It accepts 5 input fields, none of them required at the top level. Each result can carry up to 10 declared fields, including title, articleUrl, author, publishDate, category and tags. It costs $0.99 per 1,000 results on Apify's Free plan and $0.49 on the Business plan.

    What it does

    Pull articles from Apartment Therapy, a home decor and interior design publication covering decorating, DIY projects, organizing, shopping guides, house tours, and small-space living. Get the newest articles, search by keyword, or browse a specific topic.

    What this actor does

    • Fetches the newest published articles
    • Searches articles by keyword across titles, summaries, and tags
    • Browses articles within a specific topic — decorating, DIY, organizing, shopping, kitchen, bathroom, bedroom, living room, house tours, before & after, or news
    • Filters any of the above further by author name
    • Empty fields are omitted from records

    Use cases

    • Content curation — pull the latest decorating and DIY articles for a newsletter or feed
    • Trend tracking — monitor which topics (small spaces, organizing, house tours) are being published most
    • Author monitoring — follow new pieces from a specific writer
    • Research — collect a set of articles on a topic like "small apartment ideas" for reference
    • Media monitoring — keep an eye on a competitor publication's home-and-design coverage

    Questions

    Where does the data come from?

    Apartment Therapy's own published article feed — the same information available to anyone browsing the site's latest content.

    How many articles can I get?

    The feed this actor reads carries the most recently published articles (roughly the newest 20 per category); use maxItems to cap how many of those you receive. It does not go further back into the full historical archive.

    Does search cover the entire archive or just recent articles?

    Just the recent articles available in the feed — keyword matching is applied to their titles, summaries, and tags.

    Can I combine a category with a keyword search?

    Not in a single run — mode picks one approach: latest, keyword search, or category browse. authorFilter can be combined with any of the three.

    Why is tags sometimes empty?

    Not every article is tagged with additional topics beyond its primary category; when there are none, the field is omitted.

    Is this affiliated with Apartment Therapy?

    No. This is an independent actor that reads Apartment Therapy's publicly published content and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Apartment Therapy.
