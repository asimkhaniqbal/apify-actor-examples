    YouTube scrapers and data tools

    Vexaloom Labs publishes 5 Apify Actors that read YouTube. Each one answers a different question, so pick by the data you need. Each costs $0.99 per 1,000 results on the Free plan.

    Which YouTube Actor do you need?

    • YouTube Channel Video Catalog & Profile Scraper: Get a YouTube channel's profile details (subscribers, description, links, verification) together with its video, Shorts, playlist or live-stream catalog, for one channel or many at once.
    • YouTube Playlist Contents & Channel Playlist Finder: List every video in a YouTube playlist, or discover every public playlist a channel owns, with titles, durations, thumbnails, and playlist metadata.
    • YouTube Search Results & Entity Type Scraper: Search YouTube by keyword for videos, Shorts, channels, or playlists, with view counts, duration, and channel details, filterable by upload date, duration, sort order, and region.
    • YouTube Shorts Metadata Extractor for Channels & Hashtags: Pull Shorts from a channel's Shorts tab, a hashtag feed, or specific Shorts links, with views, likes, comment counts, upload dates and channel profile detail.
    • YouTube Video Metadata & Chapter Extractor: Get full metadata for any YouTube video or Short: channel info, chapters, endscreen elements, caption track list, related videos and comments, from a URL or video ID.
