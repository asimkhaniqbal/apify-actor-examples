    TikTok scrapers and data tools

    Vexaloom Labs publishes 8 Apify Actors that read TikTok. Each one answers a different question, so pick by the data you need. Each costs $0.99 per 1,000 results on the Free plan.

    Which TikTok Actor do you need?

    • TikTok Account Stats & Repost History Scraper: Look up TikTok accounts for follower/like/video stats, recent videos, and reposted content in one run. Optional session cookie unlocks a profile's full video history. No cookie required for account stats or reposts.
    • TikTok Anonymous For You Feed Snapshot Scraper: Capture a snapshot of posts currently surfacing on TikTok's anonymous For You feed, with engagement stats, author, music, and hashtag details for each video or slideshow.
    • TikTok Comment & Reply Thread Extractor: Pull comments and reply threads from TikTok videos by URL or post ID, with pinned/hearted-by-author flags, commenter details, and like counts. No TikTok account or cookies required.
    • TikTok DSA Ads Transparency Library Scraper: Search TikTok's EU/EEA ad transparency database by keyword, advertiser or ad ID. Get creative media, advertiser identity, impression buckets, audit status and per-region/age/gender targeting.
    • TikTok Keyword Search & Discovery Scraper: Search TikTok by keyword or phrase and get matching videos with engagement stats, author profiles, music metadata, hashtags, and location tags, with optional discovery expansion for more results.
    • TikTok Playlist Discovery & Video Order Scraper: Scrape TikTok playlists - discover all playlists for a profile, get playlist metadata, and extract all videos. Supports playlist URLs, IDs, and profile URLs. No cookies required.
    • TikTok Sound Lookup: Metadata & Videos Using It: Look up any TikTok sound by URL or ID and get back its title, creator, duration, cover art, and streaming links, plus every video currently using that sound with captions, hashtags, and engagement counts.
    • TikTok Video Subtitle & Transcript Extractor: Pull every subtitle track off a TikTok video - auto speech recognition plus any machine-translated languages - as timestamped segments and a plain-text transcript.
