    Naver scrapers and data tools

    Vexaloom Labs publishes 2 Apify Actors that read Naver. Each one answers a different question, so pick by the data you need. Each costs $0.99 per 1,000 results on the Free plan.

    Which Naver Actor do you need?

    • Naver Shopping Price Comparison Scraper: Search Naver Shopping by keyword, pull product details by ID, browse a category or fetch trending products, with price range across sellers, review and purchase counts, brand and mall name.
    • Naver Web, Blog, Cafe & News Results Scraper: Search Naver's web, blog, cafe, or news results for a Korean or any-language query, and get back title, URL, domain, snippet, and source name for each organic result, filterable by keyword or domain.
