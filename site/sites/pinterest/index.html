    Pinterest scrapers and data tools

    Vexaloom Labs publishes 4 Apify Actors that read Pinterest. Each one answers a different question, so pick by the data you need. Each costs $0.99 per 1,000 results on the Free plan.

    Which Pinterest Actor do you need?

    • Pinterest Media Downloader: Download public Pinterest image, GIF, Story Pin, and video media with source metadata and Key-Value Store links.
    • Pinterest Pin Comments & Activity Extractor: Pull public comments, replies, reactions, and Tried It activity for one or more Pinterest Pins, along with each Pin's title, creator, board, and engagement summary.
    • Pinterest Pins, Profiles and Boards Collector with Filters: Collect public Pinterest Pins, profiles, boards, search results and comments by keyword or URL, with filters for saves, creator followers, verification, ads and shoppable Pins.
    • Pinterest Trending Topics & Shopping Category Scraper: Collect Pinterest's public featured and editorial trend topics, its shopping category taxonomy, and shopping-category click metrics with history and forecast, by region.
