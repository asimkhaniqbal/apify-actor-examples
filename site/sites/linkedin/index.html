    LinkedIn scrapers and data tools

    Vexaloom Labs publishes 8 Apify Actors that read LinkedIn. Each one answers a different question, so pick by the data you need. Each costs $0.99 per 1,000 results on the Free plan.

    Which LinkedIn Actor do you need?

    • LinkedIn Company Page Post & Engagement Extractor: Pull posts from any LinkedIn company page with captions, media, article links, hashtags, mentions, and reaction/comment/repost counts, filterable by date and engagement.
    • LinkedIn Guest-View Profile Data Extractor: Extract names, headlines, work history, education, certifications, honors, volunteer work, projects, and follower counts from public LinkedIn profiles, with optional current-employer enrichment. No login or cookies required.
    • LinkedIn Hashtag Feed Engagement Scraper: Pull posts published under one or more LinkedIn hashtags, with author name, headline, content, media type and reaction/comment/repost counts. Works without login; a session cookie unlocks more results.
    • LinkedIn Keyword & Hashtag Post Discovery Scraper: Search LinkedIn posts by keyword or hashtag with filters for date, author type, industry, connection degree, mentions, and minimum reactions. Requires a LinkedIn session cookie.
    • LinkedIn People Search by Name with Company & School Filters: Search LinkedIn for a list of names and get matching profiles with headline, location, current role and company, connection degree, and profile picture.
    • LinkedIn Post Reactor Identity & Reaction Type Scraper: Scrape the reactors on LinkedIn posts with name, headline, profile URL, and the specific reaction type they left, filterable by reaction type and date using your own LinkedIn session.
    • LinkedIn Profile Feed Scraper with Likers & Commenters: Pull posts from a LinkedIn profile's activity feed or a direct post URL, with engagement counts, hashtags, media, and optionally the people who liked or commented on each post. Requires a LinkedIn session cookie.
    • LinkedIn Trending Posts & Top Voices Feed Scraper: Pull LinkedIn's trending and top-performing posts by topic keyword, with reaction/comment/repost counts, a per-reaction-type breakdown, author headline, and media type, so you can filter by engagement thresholds, date window, or media type.
