    Snapchat scrapers and data tools

    Vexaloom Labs publishes 4 Apify Actors that read Snapchat. Each one answers a different question, so pick by the data you need. Each costs $0.99 per 1,000 results on the Free plan.

    Which Snapchat Actor do you need?

    • Snapchat AR Lens Metadata & Related Spotlight Finder: Get trending Snapchat AR lenses, full lens details (creator, previews, related lenses, Spotlight videos using it) by URL or UUID, or lens search results by keyword. No login needed.
    • Snapchat Creator Profile & Highlights Extractor: Turn Snapchat usernames into structured profile records with bio, subscriber count, verification status, story highlights, Spotlight videos, AR lenses, and related creators.
    • Snapchat Explore Search Across Spotlight, Lenses & Places: Search Snapchat's public explore results for a keyword and get Spotlight videos, AR lenses, topics, user profiles, business profiles, publisher episodes, shows, and places in one run. No login needed.
    • Snapchat Spotlight Hashtag Video Extractor: Pull Snapchat Spotlight videos published under a hashtag or topic, with engagement stats, creator details, AI-generated descriptions and related topic tags.
