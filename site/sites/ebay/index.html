    eBay scrapers and data tools

    Vexaloom Labs publishes 2 Apify Actors that read eBay. Each one answers a different question, so pick by the data you need. Each costs $0.99 per 1,000 results on the Free plan.

    Which eBay Actor do you need?

    • eBay Item, Store & Store Category Detail Extractor: Extract detailed product data from eBay individual item pages, seller store fronts, and store category pages, or search by keyword across 8 marketplaces, with item specifics, images, seller trust signals and shipping/return terms.
    • eBay Multi-Marketplace Listing & Seller Data Scraper: Search eBay listings by keyword or category across 8 marketplaces, with titles, prices, conditions, sellers, ratings, watch counts, sold counts and shipping costs.
