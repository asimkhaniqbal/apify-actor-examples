    Facebook scrapers and data tools

    Vexaloom Labs publishes 6 Apify Actors that read Facebook. Each one answers a different question, so pick by the data you need. Each costs $0.99 per 1,000 results on the Free plan.

    Which Facebook Actor do you need?

    • Facebook Event Details & New-Event Monitor Scraper: Fetch public Facebook event details by URL, page listing, or keyword/city search, with venue GPS, hosts, ticket links, and a monitor mode that reports only newly-posted events.
    • Facebook Group Feed & Engagement Extractor: Pull posts from any public Facebook group with full text, media, reaction breakdown, top comments and reshares, plus optional group member count, admins and rules.
    • Facebook Page & Profile Finder by Keyword or URL: Find Facebook Pages or public profiles by keyword, or scrape a list of Facebook URLs directly, and get back contact info, category, ratings, follower counts, reviews, and recent posts.
    • Facebook Page Photo Gallery & Engagement Scraper: Collect photos from public Facebook pages, profiles, and albums with full-resolution image URLs, alt text, captions, reaction breakdowns, share and comment counts, and tagged people.
    • Facebook Political & Commercial Ad Transparency Extractor: Search the Facebook Ad Library by keyword, advertiser Page ID, Ad ID, or funding-entity name, and get ad text, media, run dates, platforms, and spend or impressions for political and issue ads.
    • Facebook Post & Group Comment Thread Extractor: Get comments and nested reply threads from public Facebook posts, Watch videos, photos, and group posts, with per-reaction-type counts, author details, and date filtering. No login or cookies required.
